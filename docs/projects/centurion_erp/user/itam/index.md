IT Asset Management (ITAM) is a crucial area of IT Service Management (ITSM). Our ITAM module can be manually updated of you can inventory a device and upload the report to the [API endpoint](../api.md#inventory-reports).

As part of the lifecycle management of IT Assets, You would be required to populate both accounting and ITAM. Our ITAM module is subordinate to Accounting Assets. Whenever you populate an IT Asset, Its subordinate model is also populated. This is beneficial as you only have to enter the data once and where the data is required it's already present. In this case under both Accounting Assets and IT Asset Management.
